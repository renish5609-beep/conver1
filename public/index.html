Help & Support
Voice isn't working — what do I do? +

Voice features require microphone access and work best in Chrome. Check that your browser has microphone permission for this site (click the lock icon in the address bar). If voice is still silent, try refreshing the page and clicking the mic button again.

Why isn't my data saving between sessions? +

Make sure Auto-save is toggled ON in Settings. Data saves to your account automatically after each completed session. If you're signed out, data is not saved — sign in first to enable persistence.

How is my score calculated? +

Each session is scored by AI across five dimensions: clarity, confidence, persuasion, storytelling, and conciseness. Your XP accumulates over time and determines your level (1–50). Good sessions earn more XP; a bad session never reduces your XP.

How do I delete my account? +

Email us at renishdjoshi@gmail.com with your account email and we'll delete your account and all associated data within 48 hours.
