Cookie Policy

What We Use

Conver uses browser localStorage (not traditional cookies) to store:

  • conver_token — a short-lived authentication token used during sign-in
  • conver_settings — your app preferences, accent color, and coach settings
  • conver_coach_notes — notes from your AI coach sessions
  • conver_last_session_date and conver_daily_* — for streak and daily challenge tracking
  • conver_accent — your chosen accent color theme

No Advertising or Tracking Cookies

We do not use any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies. Your data stays with you and the services listed in our Privacy Policy.

How to Clear Your Data

You can clear all locally stored Conver data at any time by going to Settings → "Clear All History" within the app, or by clearing your browser's localStorage for this site through your browser's developer tools or privacy settings.
