Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Open Conver AI and tap Sign Up. Enter your email and a password. No credit card required.

How do I delete my account?

Email appconver@gmail.com with subject "Delete My Account" and include the email on your account. We process requests within 30 days.

Why isn't the microphone working?

On iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone > enable Conver AI. On web: allow microphone access when your browser prompts you.

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email. Check your spam folder if you don't see the reset link.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is not sold to third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

The app isn't loading. What should I do?

Force-close and reopen the app. Check your internet connection. Still broken? Email appconver@gmail.com and describe what you see.
