Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Effective immediately

1. Who We Are

Conver is an AI-powered conversation coaching application built and operated by Renish Joshi. You can contact us at appconver@gmail.com with any questions about this policy.

2. What Data We Collect

When you use Conver, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: Your email address when you register for an account.
  • Session data: Text responses you submit for analysis, AI-generated scores and feedback, scenario and coach choices.
  • Usage data: Session counts, streak data, skill scores, and practice history.
  • Voice data: During Voice Sessions, audio is processed in real-time by your browser's speech recognition API. We do not store raw audio recordings — only the transcribed text and associated scores.
  • Camera data: During Real-Time video sessions, your camera feed is analyzed entirely inside your browser using MediaPipe Face Mesh — a computer vision model that runs locally on your device. Facial landmark data (used to compute eye contact, head stability, and expression metrics) is never sent to our servers or any third party. If you choose to download a session recording, that video file is generated and saved directly to your device by your browser — we never receive or store a copy of it.
  • Settings and preferences: Your chosen coach, scenario preferences, and app settings.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the Conver coaching experience
  • To analyze your responses using the Anthropic Claude API and generate feedback
  • To synthesize voice responses using the ElevenLabs API
  • To track your progress over time and surface personalized coaching
  • To maintain your account and authenticate your sessions

4. Third-Party Services

Conver uses the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Anthropic (Claude API): Your text responses are sent to Anthropic's API for AI analysis. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • ElevenLabs: Coach responses are synthesized using ElevenLabs' text-to-speech API. See ElevenLabs' Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: Your account data and session history are stored in Supabase's database infrastructure. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and session data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your session history at any time using the "Clear All History" option in Settings. To request complete account deletion, email us at appconver@gmail.com.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at appconver@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

Conver uses browser localStorage to store your session token, settings, and coach notes locally on your device. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details.

8. Children's Privacy

Conver is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Conver after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at appconver@gmail.com.
